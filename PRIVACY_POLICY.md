### Overview
This Privacy Policy describes how **Tic Tac Toe – XOXO** ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with Google Play Developer Policies and global data protection standards (GDPR, COPPA).

**Summary: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.**

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
* **No Personal Data Collected:** The app does not require registration, login, or any personal details (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, or payment information).
* **No Tracking or Analytics:** The app does not collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID, IMEI, or IP address) or usage analytics.

### 2. Local Device Storage
All gameplay records and settings are stored locally on your device via Android Jetpack DataStore:
* **Game Statistics:** Matches played, wins, losses, draws, and win percentages across difficulty levels and multiplayer modes.
* **User Preferences:** Sound effect toggle, haptic vibration toggle, visual animation toggle, and app theme (Dark / Light / System).

This data is kept exclusively within the application's private storage sandbox on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or shared with third parties.

### 3. Device Permissions
* **`android.permission.VIBRATE`**: Used exclusively to produce tactile haptic feedback during moves and game over events. Can be disabled anytime in the in-app Settings menu.
* **`android.permission.INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`**: Used by Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to load banner and interstitial ads and check connectivity.

### 4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)
* The app integrates the **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK** to serve non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements during natural match breaks.
* Google AdMob may process non-sensitive device indicators (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID, IP-derived general location, and basic hardware information) for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
* For more information on how Google manages advertising information, refer to [Google Advertising Policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
* No other tracking, analytics, or behavioral profiling SDKs are integrated.

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)
The app is safe for families and children of all ages. Because we collect zero personal data, we strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) requirements.

### 6. Data Deletion
Since we do not store your data on external servers:
* You can reset all gameplay statistics anytime via the **Reset Statistics** option in the app.
* Clearing the app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally saved data.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
