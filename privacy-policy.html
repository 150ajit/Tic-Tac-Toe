Privacy Policy

Application: Tic Tac Toe – XOXO

Summary: Tic Tac Toe – XOXO is designed with user privacy as a foundational principle. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or sensitive user data. All gameplay records and preferences remain 100% on your local device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android mobile application Tic Tac Toe – XOXO (Package ID: com.aks.tictactoeai), developed and provided by the developer ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy outlines our handling of information in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, the Google Play User Data Policy, and applicable global privacy regulations (including GDPR and COPPA).

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).

When you use Tic Tac Toe – XOXO, you are not required to create an account, register, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, location, or payment details.

3. Local Device Data Storage

The application stores minimal non-personal configuration and progress data locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore. This includes:

  • Gameplay Statistics: Number of matches played, wins, losses, draws, and win rates across single-player AI difficulties (Easy, Medium, Hard) and two-player mode.
  • User Settings: Audio preferences (sound effects on/off), haptic feedback (vibration on/off), animation preferences, and display theme (Dark, Light, or System default).

This data is stored purely within the application's isolated local sandbox storage on your device. It is never transmitted across the internet, never backed up to remote developer servers, and is never accessible to other third-party apps.

4. Device Permissions

The application requests minimal, necessary device permissions:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to provide tactile haptic vibration feedback during gameplay. You can disable haptic feedback at any time in Settings.
  • android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely by Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to retrieve and display advertisements (banner and interstitial ads) and check network connectivity.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tic Tac Toe – XOXO integrates the official Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to display banner and interstitial advertisements during natural game breaks. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device information to facilitate ad delivery, fraud prevention, and performance metrics, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device information (such as model, operating system version, and general technical specifications)
  • General location (derived from IP address, non-precise)

We do not use any other third-party analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs. To learn more about how Google uses data when you use apps with AdMob, please visit Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Tic Tac Toe – XOXO is designed for general audiences of all ages, including children and families. In strict accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR Article 8, our application does not knowingly collect, request, or retain any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored exclusively on your device:

  • You can reset your lifetime gameplay records at any time using the "Reset Statistics" button inside the app Settings.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing the app storage in your Android system settings permanently erases all locally saved preferences and statistics.

8. Security

We value your trust. The application relies on Android operating system security measures (app sandboxing and encrypted local storage) to safeguard your local preferences and records against unauthorized access.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date" at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
